About Uganda Tourism

"Explore Uganda" is a tourism website dedicated to showcasing the beauty and diversity of Uganda as a travel destination.

Our mission is to provide comprehensive information, inspiration, and resources for travelers interested in exploring Uganda's rich culture, stunning landscapes, and incredible wildlife.

History of Tourism in Uganda

The history of tourism in Uganda can be broken down into four simple chapters:

The Early Spark (1907):

Tourism began when British leader Winston Churchill visited and named Uganda the "Pearl of Africa" because of its natural beauty.

The Golden Era (1960s):

After gaining independence, Uganda became the top travel spot in East Africa. It welcomed over 100,000 international visitors every year to its booming wildlife parks.

The Collapse (1970s–1980s):

Severe political instability under military rule completely stopped tourism. Parks were abandoned, and heavy poaching wiped out large numbers of wildlife.

The Rebirth (1990s–Present):

Peaceful conditions returned, allowing the industry to rebuild. Uganda shifted focus toward forest eco-tourism, famously launching mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

Today, tourism is a thriving economic pillar driven by wildlife conservation and a growing community of certified travel operators.
