Welcome to Uganda

"The Pearl of Africa"

Uganda is one of Africa’s most beautiful tourist destinations, known for its wildlife, mountains, lakes, and friendly people. It is located in East Africa and attracts thousands of tourists every year.

From gorilla trekking in Bwindi Forest to boat cruises on the Nile River, Uganda offers unique experiences for both local and international visitors.

Tourism plays a major role in Uganda’s economy by creating jobs, promoting culture, and protecting wildlife.

Why Visit Uganda?

Uganda is a country of incredible diversity, offering travelers unforgettable wildlife, culture, and adventure. Discover what makes Uganda a must-see destination:

Unrivaled Primate Encounters

Trek to see endangered mountain gorillas in Bwindi and Mgahinga, or track chimpanzees in Kibale Forest.

Warm Culture and Hospitality

Meet friendly communities, enjoy traditional music and dance, and taste authentic Ugandan cuisine.

Diverse Landscapes and Wildlife

Explore lakes, mountains, savannas and rainforests while spotting elephants, tree-climbing lions, and over 1,000 bird species.

Adventure and Relaxation

Raft the Nile, hike the Rwenzori Mountains, or unwind on the calm shores of Lake Bunyonyi.
